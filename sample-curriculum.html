Program redesign sheet

One sheet per pilot program

Each pilot program gets a redesign sheet faculty can act on: what moves to AI, what grows, which courses change and how students are assessed.

Example sheet · Builders

Cybersecurity, associate degree

115 graduates a year · Invest tier · AI can do 73% of graduates' task time

Moves to AI

Security documentation, access changes, firewall and encryption setup, log monitoring.

Grows

Working with users after incidents, coordinating with vendors and managers, decisions under pressure.

Course changes

Labs use AI agents to configure systems; students then attack and audit those configurations. Add incident simulations with human decision points.

Assessment

Judgment-delta tasks in two core courses; audit of an AI-built network with planted flaws; oral incident debrief.

AI fluency course and assessment

Teaching and grading judgment

Four practices from our AI fluency framework run through every program, starting with one required course early in the degree.

Four practices

Bounding

Define the task and its limits before handing it over.

Allocating

Split the work between person and AI.

Auditing

Check AI output against evidence.

Withholding

Know when not to use AI at all.

The judgment-delta assessment

Four steps, used in key courses in every program

  1. Unaided attemptThe student solves the task alone.
  2. AI-assisted passThe student works with AI.
  3. AuditThe student checks AI work with planted errors.
  4. TransferA later unaided task shows the skill stuck.

Scenario-based delegation assessment available as a pilot while expert validation is completed.

Faculty development and roadmap

From workshops to redesigned programs

The engagement runs alongside the college's own approval cycle.

  1. Months 1–3

    Diagnose

    Leadership Fluency Check and debrief. Document review. Task maps for every program. Choose three pilots.

  2. Months 3–6

    Faculty workshops

    AI fluency for faculty; designing judgment-delta assessments; teaching-clinic models.

  3. Months 6–12

    Pilot

    Redesign sheets for the pilots. Launch the AI fluency course. Employer panels review student work.

  4. Year 2 onward

    Scale

    All high-AI programs adopt the five shifts. Update transfer agreements and accreditation plans. Track measures each year.

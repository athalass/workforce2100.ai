Summary

We collect only what you choose to send us, use it to respond and to deliver services, and never sell it.

1. Who is responsible

Horizon 2100 LLC operates this site and is responsible for personal information collected through it.

2. What we collect

  • Report requests and email. When you use the request form, we receive your name, email address, organization, role, state and message. They are stored in a Google Sheet in our Google account and sent to us by email, and we use them only to reply to you and prepare a proposal. We keep requests for up to two years, or as long as we have an engagement with you; ask us to delete yours at any time.

  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, records IP addresses for security and operations. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

To answer inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver and invoice services, and keep business records.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that support our business (for example email, hosting, and Google, which stores request records), when required by law, or with your permission.

5. Retention

We keep inquiry emails for 2 years and client records for as long as needed for the engagement and our legal and tax obligations.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing info@workforce2100.ai. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

7. Security

8. Children

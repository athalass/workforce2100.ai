What it means

Priorities for the state

The report closes with priorities a state can fund and track.

  1. Build care and trades pipelines.

    Home health, nursing and the construction trades add the most jobs; fund short, stackable credentials into them.

  2. Fund curriculum redesign, not just new programs.

    85% of completions are in programs where AI does half or more of graduates' work.

  3. Steer workers to destinations with room.

    Match transfer support to occupations with enough new jobs, not only the closest skill match.

  4. Fill local training gaps.

    Counties without providers for their growing occupations need new partners.

  5. Refresh the numbers every year.

    Re-run the model with new federal data and track the measures.

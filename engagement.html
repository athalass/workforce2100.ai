This page summarizes the standard terms for our reports and services. Each engagement is governed by a signed services agreement and statement of work, which control if they differ from this summary.

1. Scope

Each statement of work defines the report or service, the geography or institution covered, deliverables, timeline and fees.

2. Your report

On full payment, you may use, copy, share and publish the delivered report and data workbook for any purpose, including publishing it in full, with credit to Workforce 2100. You decide whether a report stays internal or becomes public.

3. Our methods

Horizon 2100 LLC keeps all rights in its models, code, methods, templates and underlying databases, and may use what it learns to improve them. Our published methods remain open at workforce2100.org.

4. Your data

Data you provide (for example placement or enrollment records) is confidential. We use it only to deliver your engagement, protect it with reasonable safeguards, do not publish or sell it, and return or delete it at the end of the engagement on request. We will sign a data-sharing agreement where your rules require one, including for education records covered by FERPA.

5. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, except as needed to perform the engagement or as required by law, including public-records laws that apply to public institutions.

6. Estimates, not guarantees

Forecasts are estimates under stated scenarios and assumptions and carry uncertainty, which each report shows as ranges. We do not guarantee any outcome, and decisions based on a report remain yours.

7. Third-party data

Reports use public federal data and licensed third-party sources, credited in each report. Third-party terms continue to apply to that material.

8. Fees and payment

Fees are set in each statement of work. Invoices are due within 30 days. Any deposit, cancellation or change-order terms are set in the statement of work.

9. Warranty and liability

We will perform services in a professional manner consistent with the statement of work. Except as stated, services are provided without other warranties. Our total liability under an engagement is limited to the fees paid for that engagement, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, to the extent permitted by law.

10. Independence

We do not change findings to suit a client. If a client asks to publish a report, the methods and limits sections stay with it.

11. Governing law

Maryland law governs, unless a public client's law requires otherwise.
